Enterprise Engine Radar

MindReply · September 2026

The platforms that still hold up for regulated, large-scale production. Scorecard + evaluation kit. Run it against any shortlist. Primary band is mandatory — no band, no score.

Three bands

Micro-product systems — replace one or two skilled people. Turnkey outputs: funnels, landing pages + sequences, course factories. Immediate revenue or hours returned.Tasks
Operational systems — replace departments. Workflow infrastructure: multi-turn support with SLA, brand-voice engines. Cut recurring labor. Reliable output.Departments
Enterprise engines — replace business units. Reusable business IP: SaaS generators, knowledge engines. Exclusivity, scale, team substitution, licensing.Businesses

Buyers pay for direct revenue impact, operational replacement, consistency, scarcity, and licensing. This radar scores the platforms that support those levels.
